WHAT CHANGED

Art on the left — album cover moves left, track info and controls sit to its right.
Coloured dot album art — cover is drawn as a dot-matrix halftone: each dot keeps the artwork's real colour, dimmed by a gamma curve so midtones stay calm on black. Click it to replay the load.
Loading pattern — a radial dot-matrix pulse plays while the art loads, driven per-dot by f(row, col, t) → opacity / scale.
Dot-matrix progress — the capsule seek bar becomes a row of dots; played dots are red, the playhead dot glows red.
Dot-matrix buttons — prev / play-pause / next are drawn from dots instead of solid glyphs.
Controls to top-right — shuffle, repeat, lyrics, queue and more become small dot icons in the header; no tab strip.
Bottom bar hidden — the mini-player / nav bar is gone on this screen; a single live lyric line replaces the lyrics tab.
