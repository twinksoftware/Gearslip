# Privacy policy

Gearslip belongs to Twink Software LLC ("we"). This policy says what Gearslip sends off your
phone, to whom, and why. Most of what Gearslip does stays on the phone.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

## What stays on your phone

- **Your voice.** Speech turns into text on the phone. Gearslip never records or sends what
  you say.
- **Messages, calls, and contacts.** Gearslip reads them to show and speak them in the car,
  and it never sends them anywhere.
- **The certificate.** It stays on your phone. Gearslip never uploads, backs up, or logs it.
- **Logs.** Gearslip writes a log of each connection to the phone. It leaves the phone only
  when you share it yourself, for example by attaching it to a GitHub issue.
- **Your location.** Gearslip uses it only while a car is connected, so the car's map and
  weather work, and with Allow all the time, so the map keeps working with the phone locked.
  It sends the location nowhere except to the weather service listed below.
- **What your car reports.** Speed, fuel, location, and the like go only to the apps on
  your phone that ask for them.

## Usage notes

Gearslip can send us short notes that show which cars it works in. They stay off unless you
turn them on, in setup or in Settings. Turn them off, and they stop at once.

- **What they carry.** The Gearslip version and which boxes you ticked, plus whatever those
  boxes cover: the Android version and whether the phone runs GrapheneOS, the phone model,
  whether a car connected that day, and each car's head unit, model, year, screen size, and
  connection result, with whether that drive used the cable or wireless and whether music
  went through Gearslip or over Bluetooth. If you tick it, how each drive went: how far the
  connection got, how long it lasted, and how it ended. Settings shows the exact note.
- **What they leave out.** Your name, accounts, location, contacts, messages, and anything
  you type or play.
- **How phones are counted.** A random number counts each phone once. We keep only a salted
  hash of it.
- **Your country.** Our server works out the country from the connection a note arrives on,
  and keeps the country only. It sees your IP address, as any website does, and doesn't
  store it.
- **When they go out.** When you open Gearslip, at most once a day, and after each drive.
- **Why.** To find out which cars, phones, and releases work, and to fix the ones that don't.
  We never sell the notes, show ads, or share them with anyone, and nothing in them is used
  to build a profile of you.
- **Where they're kept.** On our server, which runs on Cloudflare. Cloudflare stores the
  notes for us and doesn't use them for anything else.
- **How long.** Two years. Older notes are deleted automatically.

## Other services Gearslip contacts

Each of these gets only what it needs, and each has its own privacy policy.

| Service | When | What it receives |
|---|---|---|
| [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/en/terms) | When the car's dashboard shows the weather | Your phone's location |
| BetterLyrics (lyrics-api.boidu.dev), [LRCLIB](https://lrclib.net), and KuGou | While the lyrics panel is open, for each song that plays | The song's title, artist, album, and length |
| [GitHub](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) | Once a day, to check for an update, unless you turn checks off | A request for the latest release |
| GitHub | In setup, if you choose to download the certificate | A request for the certificate file |
| [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/privacy) | When you download a speech model | A request for the model file |
| [OpenFreeMap](https://openfreemap.org) | When an app in the car shows a list of places on a map | Requests for the map tiles of the area shown |
| [EasyList](https://easylist.to) | About once a week on Wi-Fi, while the browser's ad blocker is on | A request for the filter lists |
| Any address you give | When you download a certificate from a link, or add your own filter list | A request for that file |

Every request also carries your IP address, as any connection to the internet does.

The web browser in the car searches with the engine you pick (DuckDuckGo unless you change
it) and opens whatever sites you visit, like any browser. A bookmark's icon comes from that
site itself. The map, music, and messaging apps you use through Gearslip are other
companies' apps, with their own policies.

## Your rights

You can turn usage notes off at any time. Untick a phone detail in Settings, and our server
drops the copy it kept. To see the notes we hold for your phone, or to have them all
deleted, write to 9xgpevy3@anonaddy.me and include your phone's ID. It's the "id" line at the
top of the note in Settings, under Usage notes, and you can copy it from there. We answer
within 30 days.

If you live in the European Union or the United Kingdom, you also have the right to
complain to your data protection authority. We rely on your consent to collect usage notes,
and you can withdraw it by turning them off.

## Children

Gearslip is for drivers, not for children under 13. We don't knowingly collect anything from
them.
